Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping policies?

We offer free shipping on orders over $100. Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days, while express shipping (2-3 business days) is available for an additional fee. All orders are shipped from our facility in Portland, Oregon.

How do I return or exchange a product?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unused, unopened products in their original packaging. To initiate a return, please contact our customer service team with your order number and reason for return.

Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes, our products are formulated with sensitive skin in mind. However, we always recommend doing a patch test before full application. If you have specific concerns, please consult with our wellness team.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently, we ship to the United States and Canada. International shipping may be available for wholesale orders. Please contact us for more information about international shipping options.

How can I book a wellness consultation?

You can book a consultation through our services page or by contacting us directly. We offer both in-person and virtual consultations to accommodate your schedule and location.

Are your products cruelty-free and vegan?

Yes, all our products are cruelty-free and most are vegan. We clearly label all products that are 100% vegan. We never test on animals and are certified by Leaping Bunny.
